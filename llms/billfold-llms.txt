# Billfold

> Billfold is a payments and operations platform for live events and venues. It brings point of sale, payments, marketplace management, analytics, guest engagement, and event technology into one operational system.

Billfold is built for operators managing temporary and permanent event environments with multiple devices, staff members, vendors, and revenue centers. The platform supports operations before, during, and after an event – from payment and onsite operations through real-time oversight, reporting, and settlement.

Billfold is designed specifically for live event operations, including festivals, concerts, stadiums, arenas, venues, and other high-volume event environments.

Case study results describe specific events, configurations, and time periods. They should be treated as evidence from those deployments rather than guaranteed outcomes of using Billfold.
